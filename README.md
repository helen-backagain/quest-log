# Quest Log

Made by @helen_backagain

A personal quest log: one board, refreshed hourly by your Muse, that consolidates your email, calendars, reminders, and ongoing work into quests, and answers one question at a glance: what is the next right thing?

## Use it

Point your Muse at the recipe and say "build me this":

- Recipe (rendered): [quest-log-recipe.md](https://github.com/helen-backagain/quest-log/blob/main/quest-log-recipe.md)
- Recipe (raw, for your Muse to fetch): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/helen-backagain/quest-log/main/quest-log-recipe.md

The recipe is written for a Muse agent, not a human reader: page anatomy, the triage and priority rules, the dateline, the hourly refresh job template, the data actions, and the design language. No personal data; every board built from it differs because the person's life differs.

## Demo

`index.html` in this repo is a static demo snapshot with fictional sample data (Alex, a product designer; partner Jordan; kid Milo; dog Biscuit). Approve, Decline, and check-offs all work locally in the browser; nothing is saved. Live demo: https://helen-backagain.github.io/quest-log/ (GitHub Pages, served from this repo).
